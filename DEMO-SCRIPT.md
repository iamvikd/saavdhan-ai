# Demo script (speaker notes, 15 to 20 minute slot)

## Before the session
- Make the hosted link public (Share menu on the Claude page) or publish on GitHub Pages. Scan the QR with a phone that is not signed in to check it opens.
- Open the app on the laptop and press **Presenter mode** (larger text for the back rows). Adding `#present` to the link does the same.
- Keep a second tab with Claude or ChatGPT for the live build, with prompt 2A from `PROMPTS.md` copied.
- Volume up for read-aloud.
- Backup if Wi-Fi fails: the downloaded `index.html` runs offline on the laptop. Students can still join if they have mobile data.

## Flow

| Time | Screen | What to do and say |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00 | Open on your phone (QR) | Show the QR first so phones load while you talk. "Open this. You will need it at the end." |
| 1:00 | Why this matters | "58.6% of India shakes, 12% floods, 5,700 km of coast faces cyclones. Every state here has something." Use Punjab 2025 as the example: AI forecasts existed, so why did families still not act in time? |
| 2:30 | My State | "Which states are you from?" Ask 3 students, pick their states live, tap a hazard chip to jump to steps in their language. |
| 4:00 | Flood Hub | Open Flood Hub (needs internet), zoom to a student's state, tap a gauge. Then run the simulator: Severe, Rising, 2 to 3 days, Student volunteer. "The forecast is AI. The action is you." |
| 6:00 | Alert to Action | Already shown via My State; press Read aloud once in a regional language. "Same warning. Now a farmer's mother understands it." |
| 7:00 | Post-Flood Waste | Tap Dead animal, then Relief plastic. "The flood ends. The waste problem starts." |
| 8:30 | Live build | Paste prompt 2A into the AI tab. While it generates: "I am not typing code. I am describing a problem." Open the result. |
| 11:00 | Idea Lab | Scroll the eight gaps and the funding table. Mention the free Flood Hub API and the starter script in the repo. "Seed Fund grants up to Rs 20 lakh for proof of concept, through incubators." |
| 13:00 | Quiz | "Everyone tap Quiz. Five questions. Fastest perfect score wins." Give 2 minutes. |
| 15:00 | Winner check | Ask perfect scorers to call out their time. Take the fastest, open *Speaker: check a winner's code* on your screen, type name, score, time and code. Green means genuine. |
| 16:00 | Close | "Pick one gap. Build the prototype this week. Bring it to the poster competition or Smart India Hackathon." |

## One honest line to include
"AI removes the coding barrier. It does not remove the need to understand the problem, check the data and test with real people. That is your job."

## Prize idea
A Husk and Haven product or a certificate for the fastest perfect score.
