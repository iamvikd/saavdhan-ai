My State

Pick your state or union territory to see its main hazards. Tap a hazard to open the alert steps in your language.

Hazard lists are a general summary based on NDMA and state disaster management plans. Your State Disaster Management Authority (SDMA) publishes the detailed district-level hazard map.

Google Flood Hub, read it and act on it

Flood Hub is Google's free AI flood forecasting map. It forecasts river floods up to 7 days ahead and flash floods in dense urban areas up to 24 hours ahead. In India, many of its river gauges use Central Water Commission data.

Open Flood Hub Opens in a new tab. Zoom to your state and tap a river gauge.

How to read a gauge

Each river gauge in Flood Hub's data carries a severity level and a trend.

Extreme

Extreme flooding is forecast.

Severe

Severe flooding is forecast.

Above normal

Water is forecast above normal levels.

No flooding

No flooding is forecast.

Trend shows whether the forecast is rising, falling or stable. Gauges marked "quality verified" have models that passed Google's checks; treat the rest with more caution.

Build on the real data

Flood Forecasting API

Free access to the same live forecasts, for non-commercial use, after joining Google's waitlist. Data is licensed CC BY 4.0. The repo includes a starter script that lists every gauge in India with its latest severity and draws a map.

Open datasets

Google Runoff Reanalysis and Reforecast (river discharge, 1980 to 2023), Inundation History (how often each 128 m pixel was wet, 1999 to 2020), and Groundsource (about 2.6 million historical flood events from news reports).

Alert to Action

An alert that says "Red warning" does not tell a family what to do. Pick a hazard and a language to turn the warning into steps.

Flood steps are available in 8 languages; other hazards in English and Hindi, some in Punjabi. Is your language missing? That is a good first project with AI. Read aloud uses your device's built-in voice; most Android phones have Indian language voices, some laptops only English. Translations were drafted by AI and should be checked by a native speaker before public use. That check is part of responsible AI.

Emergency numbers: 112 (national emergency), 108 (ambulance), 101 (fire). Confirm your district control room number with the DDMA.

Household Flood Risk Estimator

A transparent teaching model. It shows how a prediction combines several signals into one score, and it explains every point it adds.

This is not an official forecast. Real systems use rainfall, river gauge, terrain and satellite data (CWC, IMD, Google Flood Hub). The point is the idea: inputs, weights, a score, and a reason for every number.

Post-Flood Waste Guide

When the water goes down, the waste stays. Tap an item to see which stream it belongs to and how to handle it safely.

India's NIDM (2023) recommends every area keep a pre-disaster solid waste contingency plan. After the Manipur floods, one processing plant received 2.5 to 3 times its normal waste. Segregation at source is what keeps a flood from becoming a disease outbreak.
